We are redesigning the Market Analytics Terminal into a recruiter-first Sales & Trading / Cross-Asset Risk workstation.

The goal is NOT to add as many features as possible. The goal is to transform the application from a collection of long analytical pages into a coherent desk-style terminal where market context, current portfolio risk, scenario losses, sensitivities and potential hedges are immediately visible.

Preserve mathematically correct existing engines and tests whenever possible. Refactor presentation/state architecture rather than unnecessarily rewriting financial calculations.

## 1. GLOBAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

The terminal must:

- work immediately with a default demo book;
- not require a CSV upload before showing useful analytics;
- use charts/KPIs as the primary communication layer;
- use dataframes mainly for editable books, audit detail or raw-data expanders;
- expose methodology and formulas on demand rather than filling the main screen;
- avoid duplicated inputs across modules;
- use one shared position book and one shared market state;
- support English and French globally;
- support custom light and dark modes globally;
- maintain Excel exports;
- retain CSV import but demote it to an optional "Load custom book" action;
- remain explicitly educational/proxy analytics and never claim bank-grade pricing or regulatory compliance.

The design should feel closer to a modern institutional market/risk terminal than to a collection of student calculators.

Avoid excessive rounded cards, decorative Apple-style UI, unnecessary explanatory text and long vertical pages.

## 36. FEATURES TO DEFER

## FINAL PRODUCT STANDARD

A recruiter opening the application should understand within approximately 30 seconds:

1. what markets are doing;
2. what portfolio/book is loaded;
3. where the major risk concentrations are;
4. what the worst scenarios are;
5. how derivative/rates/FX exposures behave;
6. what hedge or trade expression can address that exposure.

The terminal should communicate:

Market → Position → Risk → Scenario → Hedge → Decision.

That chain is the central product principle for the V2.
